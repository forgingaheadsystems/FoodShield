# FoodShield-to-Industry Terminology Map

**Name status:** Owner-approved FoodShield terminology. The full name was
proposed by Codex and approved by the owner on 2026-11-12. “Terminology map” is
plain-language project wording; “crosswalk” is an established external term for
some forms of concept or classification mapping, but it is not used here as an
unstated requirement.

**Status:** Initial terminology audit; not an applicability or compliance
determination.

This map keeps FoodShield language interoperable with government programs,
funding, trucking, warehousing, produce trade, food safety, and supply-chain
standards. An external term applies only in the scope established by its owning
authority. The same organization may perform several regulated functions, and
the same ordinary word may designate different concepts in different systems.

## Mapping rules

The labels **Keep**, **Distinguish**, and **Unresolved** are agent-proposed audit
labels adopted for this glossary task; they are not terms required by the cited
authorities.

Plain-language comparison labels marked **agent descriptor** below were written
by Codex only to align concepts in the table. They are not source terminology or
approved FoodShield vocabulary.

- **Keep:** preserve the external authority's exact term when that system is in
  scope.
- **Distinguish:** do not merge terms that have different functions, legal
  relationships, records, or authorities.
- **Unresolved:** preserve the question without assigning a FoodShield meaning.

## Charitable food programs

Authority: [7 CFR 251.3 — Definitions](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/subtitle-B/chapter-II/subchapter-B/part-251/section-251.3).

| FoodShield or ordinary expression | External term and authority | Disposition | Required distinction |
|---|---|---|---|
| food pantry | `food pantry` — USDA TEFAP, 7 CFR 251.3 | Keep; context-qualified | The TEFAP definition controls only when that program is in scope. Do not infer TEFAP participation from the ordinary label. |
| food bank | `food bank` — USDA TEFAP, 7 CFR 251.3 | Keep; context-qualified | A food bank is not interchangeable with a food pantry, warehouse, carrier, or funder even if it performs those functions. |
| **Agent descriptor:** participating food organization | `eligible recipient agency` and `emergency feeding organization` — USDA TEFAP, 7 CFR 251.3 | Distinguish | These are TEFAP program roles, not generic names for every pantry or food bank. `Emergency feeding organization` is not the same concept as the survey's “nearby emergency services.” |

## Federal funding and awards

Authorities: [2 CFR 200.1 — Definitions](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/subtitle-A/chapter-II/part-200/subpart-A/section-200.1) and [2 CFR 200.331 — Subrecipient and contractor determinations](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/subtitle-A/chapter-II/part-200/subpart-D/section-200.331).

| FoodShield or ordinary expression | External term and authority | Disposition | Required distinction |
|---|---|---|---|
| recipient | `recipient` — 2 CFR 200.1 | Distinguish | In Federal-award records, this means the entity receiving an award directly from a Federal agency; it does not mean a person receiving pantry food. |
| **Agent descriptor:** downstream funded organization | `subrecipient` and `pass-through entity` — 2 CFR 200.1 | Keep when applicable | A subrecipient carries out part of a Federal award. A pass-through entity provides a subaward. The relationship, not a casual label, controls. |
| **Agent descriptor:** vendor or service provider | `contractor` — 2 CFR 200.1 and the determination in 2 CFR 200.331 | Distinguish | A procurement relationship is not automatically a subaward relationship. Classification requires the actual agreement and functions. |
| **Agent descriptor:** program participant | `participant` — 2 CFR 200.1 | Distinguish | This Federal-award term excludes people responsible for implementing the award and must not silently replace FoodShield's unresolved user-facing term for people receiving food. |

## Food transportation and trucking

Authorities: [21 CFR 1.904 — Sanitary transportation definitions](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/chapter-I/subchapter-A/part-1/subpart-O/section-1.904), [FDA sanitary-transportation FAQs](https://www.fda.gov/food/food-safety-modernization-act-fsma/frequently-asked-questions-fsma), and [FMCSA's motor-carrier, broker, and freight-forwarder definitions](https://www.fmcsa.dot.gov/faq/what-are-definitions-motor-carrier-broker-and-freight-forwarder-authorities).

| FoodShield or ordinary expression | External term and authority | Disposition | Required distinction |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Agent descriptor:** party arranging food transport | `shipper` — FDA sanitary transportation rule | Distinguish | In this FDA context, the shipper arranges transportation; it need not be the vehicle operator or the location physically sending the food. |
| **Agent descriptor:** party loading food | `loader` — FDA sanitary transportation rule | Keep when applicable | This is a function performed during a transportation operation, not necessarily a separate company. |
| **Agent descriptor:** party physically moving food | `carrier` — FDA sanitary transportation rule | Distinguish | This FDA food-safety function is not automatically identical to every FMCSA motor-carrier classification. |
| **Agent descriptor:** party receiving transported food | `receiver` — FDA sanitary transportation rule | Distinguish | This term is function- and rule-specific. It must not be merged with a produce-contract receiver or a Federal-award recipient. |
| **Agent descriptor:** trucking business | `motor carrier` — FMCSA | Keep when applicable | A motor carrier physically transports property in commerce. Do not call a broker or freight forwarder a motor carrier unless that role is actually performed and authorized. |
| **Agent descriptor:** party arranging motor-carrier transportation | `broker` — FMCSA | Distinguish | An FMCSA broker arranges transportation and does not transport or assume responsibility for the cargo in the broker role. This differs from a PACA produce broker. |
| **Agent descriptor:** party consolidating and assuming transportation responsibility | `freight forwarder` — FMCSA | Distinguish | A freight forwarder may transport freight and assumes transportation responsibility; it is not synonymous with broker. Multiple roles can require multiple registrations. |

FDA states that one entity performing more than one sanitary-transportation
function must meet the requirements for each covered function. **Inference from
that source:** these external roles can vary by operation rather than acting as
one permanent organization category. Whether or how FoodShield records that
variation remains unresolved.

## Warehousing, traceability, and movement events

Authority: [21 CFR 1.1310 — Food Traceability Rule definitions](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/chapter-I/subchapter-A/part-1/subpart-S/section-1.1310).

| FoodShield or ordinary expression | External term and authority | Disposition | Required distinction |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Agent descriptor:** storage or warehousing | `holding` — FDA Food Traceability Rule, 21 CFR 1.1310 | Distinguish | FDA's term includes food storage and certain activities incidental to storage. “Warehouse” is a facility description, not a universal regulatory role. |
| **Agent descriptor:** receipt event | `receiving` — 21 CFR 1.1310 | Distinguish | This traceability event requires receipt by someone other than a consumer after transport from another location. It is not identical to every inventory receipt. |
| **Agent descriptor:** shipment event | `shipping` — 21 CFR 1.1310 | Distinguish | In this rule, shipping excludes direct-to-consumer sale or shipment and donation of surplus food. FoodShield must not apply this controlled meaning to every ordinary use of “shipping.” |
| **Agent descriptor:** transport-only custodian | `transporter` — 21 CFR 1.1310 | Distinguish | This is possession, custody, or control solely for transportation; it is not automatically the FDA sanitary-transportation carrier or FMCSA motor carrier. |
| **Agent descriptor:** batch or lot | `traceability lot` and `traceability lot code` — 21 CFR 1.1310 | Distinguish | An internal inventory batch is not automatically an FDA traceability lot, and an internal identifier is not automatically a traceability lot code. |

## Produce trade

Authorities: [USDA PACA Common Terms](https://www.ams.usda.gov/rules-regulations/paca/common-terms) and [PACA Licensing](https://www.ams.usda.gov/rules-regulations/paca/licensing).

| FoodShield or ordinary expression | External term and authority | Disposition | Required distinction |
|---|---|---|---|
| acceptance, receiver, shipper, delivered, F.O.B., good delivery | PACA trade and contract terms — USDA Agricultural Marketing Service | Keep only in applicable produce transactions | These terms allocate contract rights, risk, rejection, or condition in produce commerce. Do not reuse them as general inventory-status labels without qualification. |
| dealer, broker, commission merchant, growers' agent | PACA roles — USDA Agricultural Marketing Service | Unresolved until facts establish applicability | PACA licensing and role classification depend on actual transactions and thresholds. A generic label such as “produce supplier” does not establish any of these roles; FoodShield's future verification and recordkeeping method is unresolved. |

## Supply-chain identifiers and exchange standards

Authorities: [GS1 US Standards at a Glance](https://www.gs1us.org/industries-and-insights) and [FDA Food Traceability Rule FAQs](https://www.fda.gov/food/food-safety-modernization-act-fsma/frequently-asked-questions-fsma-food-traceability-rule).

| FoodShield or ordinary expression | External term and authority | Disposition | Required distinction |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Agent descriptor:** product identifier | `GTIN` — GS1 | Keep if adopted or supplied by a trading partner | Do not relabel a FoodShield-only item ID as a GTIN. |
| **Agent descriptor:** location identifier | `GLN` — GS1 | Keep if adopted or supplied by a trading partner | Do not relabel an address, pantry number, or FoodShield location ID as a GLN. |
| **Agent descriptor:** logistics-unit identifier | `SSCC` — GS1 | Keep if adopted or supplied by a trading partner | A pallet or case ID is not automatically an SSCC. |
| **Agent descriptor:** product-event exchange | `EPCIS` — GS1 | Unresolved | GS1 can support interoperability, but FDA does not prescribe one technology or standard for traceability. Trading-partner and operational needs must be established first. |

## Open owner decisions

- The user-facing FoodShield term for people or households receiving food.
- Which organizations and programs will be initial FoodShield users.
- Whether FoodShield will record regulated roles, verify them, or merely exchange
  role data supplied by an organization.
- Which transportation, funding, food-safety, produce-trade, and traceability
  regimes apply to each initial workflow.
- Which external identifiers or exchange standards actual partners require.

These decisions are deliberately not converted into implementation requirements
by this map.
